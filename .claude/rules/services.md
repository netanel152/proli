# Service layer (`app/services/`) and the provider facade (`app/providers/whatsapp/`)

Loaded when a file under `app/services/` or `app/providers/` is read. The narrative
version is `docs/ARCHITECTURE.md`; the prescriptive one is `docs/ARCHITECTURE_STANDARD.md`.
The three-process overview and the data layer stay in `CLAUDE.md`.

Two rules from `CLAUDE.md` bind every file here and are repeated because they are the
ones an edit in this directory can break: **every WhatsApp menu is text-only** (nothing
calls `send_interactive`), and **all outbound traffic goes through `app/providers/whatsapp/`**
— no `httpx`/`requests` import under `app/services/` (allowlist: `geocoding_service.py`),
no `CloudAPIProvider(`/`DryRunProvider(` construction outside the facade package. CI greps
for both.

`customer_flow.py`, `pro_flow.py` and the guard chain receive their collaborators (`whatsapp`,
`lead_manager`, `state_manager`, …) **as parameters** — `workflow_service.py` passes its shared
instances — so a flow function is testable against fakes without monkeypatching. Helpers that
still live in `workflow_service` are resolved per call through the module, never imported at
module top, because the suite monkeypatches them late.

| `conversation_pipeline.py` | `run_customer_pipeline(ctx, deps)` — everything a message reaches once no guard in `GUARD_CHAIN` handles it (PRO-182, PRO-139 slice B): the inbound log, the completion/rating/review branch, media fetch, the active-lead lookup, the PRO-116 Q3 BOOKED gate, the fresh-start context clear, the PRO-215 wrong-trade check (an assigned pro whose trade doesn't fit the issue, unless the lead has `assigned_by_admin_at`, is dropped: `pro_id` cleared, a CONTACTED lead falls through to the dispatcher and matching, a NEW lead goes to `monitor_service.reassign_lead(notify_old_pro=False)`; a vague issue is read with `trade_named_in_chat` and only the trade's label is recorded; the drop is conditional on pro and status, removes `quoted_price`, and sends `Pro.LEAD_OUTSIDE_TRADE`/`LEAD_OUTSIDE_TRADE_OFFER` and `Customer.MOVING_TO_FITTING_PRO`; a NEW lead records the turn's media/emergency before `reassign_lead`, escalates with `wrong_trade_rematch_failed` if it raises, and pages on an emergency with no replacement; an issue with no known trade that reaches matching escalates with `unknown_trade`, and an emergency with a city reaches matching even with no issue), the assigned-pro fast path, the Smart Dispatcher phase, the sticky persistence gate, PRO-119 address-parts persistence, the loyalty check (scans up to `LOYALTY_HISTORY_SCAN_LIMIT` recent COMPLETED leads and offers the most recent active pro whose trade fits the issue; no offer if the issue names a trade nobody in the history has, or names no trade at all), the matching gate, response selection, and `[DEAL]` detection/finalize. | `customer_flow.py` | Customer completion checks (capped + cooled-down per booking — `reassign_lead` clears the counters on a fresh owner, PRO-45; `"2"`/`עדיין לא` acknowledges and restarts the cooldown instead of falling through to the AI; `"3"`/no-show phrasing → `_handle_no_show_report`, PRO-45 and the only production writer of `no_show_count` — atomically claims the BOOKED lead within `NO_SHOW_REPORT_MAX_AGE_HOURS` of the check (or, for written phrasing, once the appointment is due), records the no-show, releases the slot, notifies the pro, pages the operator, and hands off to `monitor_service.reassign_lead(lead, notify_old_pro=False)`); ratings via a tolerant free-text `parse_rating` (digits, Hebrew number words, stars, "X מתוך 5") with a bounded re-prompt (`MAX_RATING_REPROMPTS`) on an unreadable reply and a 48h liveness window (`RATING_PROMPT_MAX_AGE_HOURS`) on the prompt itself (PRO-122); reviews, including an explicit skip path (`Messages.Keywords.SKIP_TOKENS`) that keeps the numeric rating but drops the comment; and rescheduling (writes both `appointment_time` and `appointment_datetime` on a new slot, PRO-45) |
